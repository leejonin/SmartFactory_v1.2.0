Converted files (STEP → GLB with occt-import-js 0.0.x, then merged by colour,
Z-up mm → Y-up m). Changes: colours were assigned or replaced as noted.
- uno.glb          ← Computing/SingleBoardComputers/Arduino/freaduino-uno.step
                     (white PCB faces recoloured to Arduino blue, yellow pins to gold)
- sg90.glb         ← Electrical Parts/Servos/SG-90/Servo-sg90.step (coloured blue)
- sg90_horn.glb    ← Electrical Parts/Servos/SG-90/SG90-1-arm-horn.step (coloured white)
- prox_sensor.glb  ← Electronics Parts/Inductors/Inductive_sensor/Inductive-Proximity-Sensor.step
                     (body recoloured yellow to resemble the kit's IR proximity sensor)
- bearing_608.glb  ← Mechanical Parts/Bearings/608ZZ_Ball_Bearing.step
- profile_2020.glb ← Mechanical Parts/Profiles EN/Generic/Profile-20x20-B-Type_Slot6_A.step

Converter: occt-import-js (https://github.com/kovacsv/occt-import-js, LGPL-2.1),
used only at conversion time; it is not shipped with this project.

Note (2026-11-10): uno.glb is no longer used by the app (the board is now modelled
in src/conveyor/arduino.js as a standard Arduino UNO R3). The file is kept unchanged.
